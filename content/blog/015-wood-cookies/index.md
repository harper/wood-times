title: Wood Cookies

When my parents oak tree came down at their old house, I saw an opportunity. Cross-sections of a tree - "wood cookies" - make beautiful display pieces, showing off the growth rings and natural character that you'd never see in dimensional lumber.

![Stump](./stump.png)

The stump told the story of a mature tree, decades of growth visible in those concentric rings. A few thick slices were cut with a chainsaw before the rest was hauled away.

![Rough Cookie](./rough_cookie.png)

Back in the shop, the raw slices were impressive but rough. Chainsaw marks, uneven thickness, and bark still attached. These would need some serious work to become usable.

![Pair](./pair.png)

Two cookies of similar size would make a matched pair. The grain patterns were unique to each slice, captured at slightly different heights along the trunk.

![Flattening](./flattening.png)

Flattening cookies this size required a lot of work, a power planer, hand planer, and a ton of sanding. 

These cookies would eventually become side tables, but that's a project for another day. For now, they needed time to stabilize and dry properly. Rushing would only lead to cracks.s
